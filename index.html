Player Rules and Survival Guide

You are not alone. The house is watching you.
You have entered a place where sounds can lie, memories can change, and not every exit leads to safety. Survive all five levels, solve the mysteries, and find a way out.

Your objectives
1. Explore each level and find the clues you need.
2. Solve puzzles and unlock the way forward.
3. Avoid traps and learn how the creatures behave.
4. Manage your flashlight, time, and other limited resources.
5. Watch for changes in rooms, objects, and sounds.
6. Reach the final level and make your escape.

Rules of survival
Rule 1. Look carefully. Objects, pictures, shadows, and doors may change when you are not looking.
Rule 2. Listen before you move. Footsteps and voices can reveal danger, but some sounds may be imitations.
Rule 3. Not every door is safe. Look for clues before entering suspicious rooms.
Rule 4. Watch your step. Some floorboards and objects can trigger traps or attract unwanted attention.
Rule 5. Use your resources wisely. Your flashlight and other items may be limited. Save them for when they matter.
Rule 6. Hiding is not always safety. Creatures may check familiar hiding places. Be ready to change your plan.
Rule 7. Learn the patterns. Every threat has habits you can watch and use.
Rule 8. Mistakes have a cost. Wrong choices may cost time, resources, or a safe route. Adapt.
Rule 9. Not every scare is a threat. Some frightening events reveal clues. Think before you react.
Rule 10. Trust evidence, not panic. When something feels wrong, compare what you see, hear, and remember.

Your five-level journey
Level 1: Observe the house.
Level 2: Solve the lies.
Level 3: Survive the hunt.
Level 4: Separate reality from memory.
Level 5: Use everything you have learned.

Remember
The game rewards careful looking, clever choices, and learning from mistakes. You do not need to find everything at once. Explore, think, adapt, and survive. When the house goes quiet, pay attention.
